Introduction


Saman Zameni is a researcher in privacy-preserving wireless systems. He holds an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering (Software) from the University of Tehran and a B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applications from Amirkabir University of Technology.

His research examines how the infrastructure behind dynamic spectrum sharing can leak sensitive information. In his master's thesis, he showed that the maximum transmit power responses returned by geolocation databases in cognitive radio networks carry spatial information about protected primary users, and evaluated how well a particle filter and supervised machine learning models can exploit it. Since 2022, in independent research building on this work, he has extended the attack into a full simulation study. He has also developed DP-MTP, a response-level differential privacy mechanism with provably near-optimal power loss, and analysed both its guarantees and its limits against coalitions of adaptive querying devices.

Alongside his research, Saman has worked in industry since 2018, first as a front-end developer building Angular applications and currently as a Software Quality Assurance Engineer for core banking systems at Informatics Services Corporation. This experience grounds his interest in how data-driven systems are designed, deployed and secured in practice.
