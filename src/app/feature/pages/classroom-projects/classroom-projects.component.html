• Research Assistant / Graduate Researcher | University of Tehran
Supervisor: Prof. Behnam Bahrak
Description: Investigated privacy vulnerabilities in Cognitive Radio Networks (CRNs) by simulating location inference attacks on Geolocation Databases (GDBs) using Maximum Transmit Power (MTP) responses. Developed a Bayesian recursive tracking framework (Particle Filtering) and supervised Machine Learning models (ANNs, SVMs) to evaluate spatial profiling threats, achieving high localization accuracy. Designed and implemented a Differential Privacy (DP) framework using calibrated Laplace noise to mitigate location inference attacks, increasing adversary RMSE to >50 meters while maintaining spectrum efficiency.
